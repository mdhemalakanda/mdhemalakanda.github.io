    Getting Started

    Installation

    Install TeamzLab FedEx Shipping on your WooCommerce store and confirm it is active.

    Watch the 60-second walkthrough, then follow the written steps below.

    ​
    Before you install

    Check three things before you start: your store, your hosting, and your FedEx account. This takes about five minutes and prevents almost every setup problem.

    ​
    Your store

    The plugin runs inside WooCommerce, so WooCommerce needs to be installed, active, and actually set up — not just present.
    • WordPress 6.x or newer, with WooCommerce installed and activated. The plugin is tested with WooCommerce up to version 11.2.
    • At least one product added. You will need it to place a test order later.
    • At least one shipping zone that covers the places you ship to. A shipping zone is a WooCommerce rule that decides which shipping options a customer is offered, based on where they are. You can review yours under WooCommerce → Settings → Shipping.
    • Your store address entered under WooCommerce → Settings → General. This matters more than it looks: FedEx calculates prices and prints labels for packages that start at a real physical address, and that address is your shop. You will enter it again in the plugin’s own settings on the next page, so the two should match.

    ​
    Your hosting

    The plugin needs PHP 7.4 or newer. PHP is the language WordPress runs on, and your host almost always handles this for you. To check yours, go to Tools → Site Health in your WordPress admin and open the Info tab — the PHP version is listed under Server. If it is older than 7.4, ask your host to update it. Hosts do this on request, usually at no cost.

    ​
    Your FedEx account

    You need a FedEx shipping account — the account your packages are billed to. If you already ship with FedEx, you have one, and the account number is printed on any FedEx invoice. You will use this account on the next page to create a free set of test credentials.

    ​
    Install the plugin

    • From WordPress.org
    • From a ZIP file
    1

    Open the plugin installer

    Go to WordPress Admin → Plugins → Add New.
    2

    Search for the plugin

    Enter Teamzlab Fedex in the search box.
    3

    Install it

    Click Install Now on the card titled Teamzlab Fedex (the plugin’s name on the Plugins screen). WordPress downloads and installs the plugin, and the button changes to Activate.
    4

    Activate it

    Click Activate.
    If you received the plugin as a ZIP file (for example teamzlab-fedex.zip), do not unzip it first.
    1

    Open the uploader

    Go to WordPress Admin → Plugins → Add New and click Upload Plugin at the top of the page.
    2

    Choose the file

    Click Choose File and choose the teamzlab-fedex.zip file from your computer.
    3

    Install it

    Click Install Now and wait for WordPress to finish.
    4

    Activate it

    Click Activate Plugin.
    The Plugins list, with Teamzlab Fedex active.

    ​
    What you should see after activation

    A new menu, FedEx Shipping, appears in the left sidebar of your WordPress admin, with an airplane icon. Under it you will find:
    • Shipments & Printings — every shipment you create, with its labels.
    • Pickup History — the pickups you schedule through the plugin.
    • Product Box — where you save your box sizes and match products to boxes.
    • Settings — where you connect your FedEx account.
    Two details worth knowing:
    • Your WordPress dashboard also gains a Recent Shipments widget that lists your latest shipments.
    • If you later switch the shipping mode to Advanced (a choice under Settings → Shipping), the Shipments & Printings menu is replaced by Open Shipments. You do not need to think about this now — the default mode suits most stores.

    ​
    Your storefront does not change yet

    The plugin changes nothing your customers can see until two things happen:
    1. You save FedEx credentials — see Connecting FedEx credentials.
    2. You add the Fedex Shipping method to at least one shipping zone — see Adding FedEx to a shipping zone.
    Until then, checkout looks and behaves exactly as before. Nothing is written to your FedEx account either — the plugin only talks to FedEx once credentials are saved and an order actually checks out.
    If you deactivate or delete the plugin later, your customers are not left without shipping: WooCommerce automatically falls back to the other methods in your zones. Deactivating keeps your settings and shipment records. Deleting the plugin removes its data — see Uninstalling.

    ​
    What the plugin stores on your site

    Three kinds of data, all visible inside your WordPress admin:
    • Your settings — everything you enter on the Settings page is kept together in one settings record in your WordPress database.
    • Shipments — each shipment is saved as a private record in your WordPress admin. Only logged-in store managers can see these; visitors and search engines cannot.
    • Labels — label PDFs are stored in wp-content/uploads/fedex-labels/ (return labels in fedex-return-labels/). This is a protected folder inside your site’s uploads area that WordPress manages for you; its files are not publicly accessible.
